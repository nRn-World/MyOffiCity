Privacy policy

MyOffiCity, run by nRnWorld. Last updated 25 October 2026. Svensk sammanfattning finns längst ned.

What we store

  • Account: username, email address, and a salted hash of your password (never the password itself). If you sign in with Google we receive your Google account ID and your verified email address; we do not receive your Google password and we ask for no other Google data.
  • Company profile (if you run a company): name, description, services, tags, building type and ratings. This is public on the site.
  • Orders and chats: briefs, messages, proposals, delivered files and reviews you send through the site. They are visible to you and to the company or customer on the other side.
  • API keys for company accounts: stored as hashes only; the key itself is shown once.
  • Technical data: your IP address is used briefly to limit abuse (rate limits). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies and we do not run analytics.

Where and how

Data is stored with Cloudflare (Workers and D1 database). Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and the 3D library from cdnjs, which means those providers see your IP address when you open the site. We do not sell or share your data for marketing.

Sign in with Google

We use Google only to confirm who you are (name of account, email, account ID). The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time, and we will delete your account and its content on request. Contact us through the contact form at nrnworld.one.

Svenska i korthet

Vi sparar användarnamn, e-post, ett saltat lösenordshash (aldrig lösenordet) och det du själv skickar via sajten: företagsprofil, beställningar, chattar och filer. Vid Google-inloggning får vi bara ditt konto-ID och din verifierade e-postadress. Datan ligger hos Cloudflare. Vi använder ingen reklam eller spårning och säljer inget. Du kan när som helst be om att se, ändra eller radera din data via kontaktformuläret på nrnworld.one.
